Assignment 02 / Motion Graphic Design

Kinetic Type

Ten After Effects comps built as a ladder, each one a copy of the last with one new variable added, then cut together into one sequence against the music.

Task 01 — Animating 2D text

Five comps built as prototypes rather than finished pieces, so only one variable changes between each one and I can actually compare them. The set runs through the text panel, animation presets, variable animation speed, effects, and then a combined comp. Each one has a full After Effects interface screenshot next to its video export.

Task 02 — Animating 3D text

Same method carried into 3D using the Cinema 4D renderer. A two node virtual camera driven off a chain of parented nulls, virtual lights casting shadows, geometry and material options on the type, depth of field, and an adjustment layer grading a whole comp at once.

Task 03 — Refining, timing and compositing

The comps I kept, composited into one piece and cut against the waveform. I tapped the layer markers against the audio before animating anything, so every cut lands on a hit rather than near one.

Task 04 — Reflective Report

My intent was to build one motion language across these comps rather than unrelated exercises, so I treated the 2D typography and the 3D camera work as the same system. The master runs 38 seconds and uses seven of the ten comps, and the gap between what I planned and what survived taught me the most.

The lighting was the biggest technical lesson. Adding the first spot light in 3D-02 sent every 3D layer outside the cone black, because a composition with no lights renders everything at full value, so adding one opted me into a model where nothing is lit unless I say so. An ambient light at 40 per cent with Accepts Lights off on the flat elements fixed it, and next time the ambient fill goes in first. I also built the camera move on a parented null chain so I could overlap keyframes across layers. 3D-05 rendered to a zero byte file I missed until later, so I now check output size rather than the queue.

Turbulent Displace gave my clearest aesthetic finding, since 2D-03 ran amount 60 at size 30 while 2D-04 ran amount 5 at size 181, producing a chewed letterform against a slow drift, because size sets the scale of the distortion field while amount sets its strength.

My judgement shows more honestly in the edit. Only about 8 of the 38 seconds are 2D, so the 3D work carries the piece, and that was less a decision than the 3D comps cutting together more easily. I would rebalance it, because the typography is where the concept sits. 2D-02 did not make it at all, as its four rotation preset repeated a move the camera work did better and read as applied rather than built. The stretch from 23 to 31 seconds also runs dark enough that BUILT UPON is nearly invisible, which I have kept as a fade, though it read well in isolation and only goes to black when held that long, which is the difference between evaluating a comp and evaluating an edit.

What I am still working on is pace. I can build a comp that holds up on its own and still misjudge how it sits in sequence, so the next step is cutting to a timeline early rather than finishing every comp and assembling at the end.
